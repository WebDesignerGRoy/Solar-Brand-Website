How solar works, in four steps.

From sunlight on your roof to power in your home or business.

  1. 1

    Sunlight reaches the panels

    Panels on your roof or on the ground catch the daylight.

  2. 2

    The panels generate electricity

    Solar cells turn light into direct current (DC) electricity.

  3. 3

    An inverter makes it usable

    The inverter converts DC into alternating current (AC), the type your appliances use.

  4. 4

    Your property uses the power

    You use solar energy first. A battery can store extra for later, if you add one.

Still connected to the grid? In most systems, yes. When your panels make less than you use, the grid tops up the difference.

What solar can do for a property.

The practical benefits, without the hype. Your own numbers come first.

Energy independence

Generating your own power during the day reduces how much you rely on the grid. A battery can stretch that into the evening.

Potential electricity-bill savings

Power you make and use yourself is power you do not buy. How much you save depends on your usage, your tariff and how well the system suits your roof, so we model your numbers before you decide.

Renewable energy use

Sunlight is a renewable source, and a solar system produces no emissions while it generates electricity.
